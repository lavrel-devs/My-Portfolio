02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Intentional by design.

I sit at the intersection of technology, creativity and problem-solving.

I'm Goodnews Daniel, a Chemistry undergraduate, digital product builder and visual designer based in Nigeria. My work moves between the visual and the technical: shaping how a brand looks, how a website behaves and how a digital product helps people get things done.

I enjoy taking an idea from a rough thought to something people can actually see, use and experience. I approach every project with curiosity, a willingness to learn and an eye for the details that make the difference.

Beyond the screen, I'm interested in education, entrepreneurship and the ways technology can create more useful opportunities.
